Itihas Studio

Itihas Studio is a private, single-user tool that the owner of the YouTube channels Itihas Untold and Tech ki Tapri runs on their own computer. It shows the owner the public statistics and YouTube Analytics of their own channels and videos (views, watch time, retention) in one local dashboard, to plan future videos.

It requests read-only access (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly). It does not upload, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to other users.
